Research · Paper in preparation

RSTAD: a self-supervised anomaly detector for retail time series

A label-free detector for retail demand, designed to catch the anomalies that memory-based models reconstruct too well and miss.

Retail signals have strong seasonality, constant promotions and almost no labeled anomalies. RSTAD learns normal behavior without labels using a Transformer masked autoencoder, then scores each window on two branches: how badly the model reconstructs it, and how far its latent representation sits from a k-nearest-neighbor memory bank of normal patterns.

The second branch targets a known weakness of memory-based detectors such as MEMTO: a decoder that generalizes too well and reconstructs anomalies as convincingly as normal data. RSTAD is benchmarked on SMD, SMAP, MSL and NAB against TFMAE, MEMTO and other recent baselines.

  • MLflow
  • Transformer masked autoencoders
  • kNN density estimation

Responsible AI · 6 audits

Responsible AI audits

Six audits of real and realistic AI systems, each pairing an empirical probe with the ethics literature and the law: the EU AI Act, GDPR and the NIST AI RMF.

Audits and their main findings
Recidivism risk model Measured racial error-rate disparity in a COMPAS-like model. Applying Equal Opportunity cut the disparity by 85% at a cost of 0.63 points of accuracy.
AI hiring tool Formal impact assessment of a candidate-ranking system. Found a GDPR Article 14 notice violation, proxy-driven scoring and an EU AI Act high-risk classification.
Census income model Counterfactual experiments showed two proxy features, capital gain and marital status, drove a misprediction.
AI research assistant Checked a NotebookLM summary claim by claim against its source and found six distortions, from an overstated consensus to a misattributed chapter.
LLM moral status Probed two frontier models side by side on six personhood criteria. Convincing moral reasoning showed competence, not moral status.
AI and labor Compared AI-exposure tools across occupations, audited a contractor reduction and drafted a worker-centric AI policy with enforceable triggers.

Open source · TypeScript · Releasing on npm

mcp-guardrail

An open-source proxy that strips secrets and personal data from AI agents' tool results before the model ever sees them.

AI agents pass tool results straight into a model's context, and those payloads often carry API keys, tokens and personal data. mcp-guardrail sits between an MCP client and any MCP server as a stdio proxy and redacts secrets and PII before they reach the model, without changes to either side.

Data infrastructure

A snapshot-based analytical layer for dashboards

A DuckDB snapshot serving path that moves heavy scans offline and takes dashboard load off production databases.

Dashboards that query raw Parquet on every request get slower as data grows. I prototyped a serving path that builds versioned DuckDB snapshots from partitioned Parquet, publishes them with a manifest, and serves queries through FastAPI, so the API never scans Parquet per request. I validated it with an async Python load harness, stepped Locust load tests and a server-timing middleware that breaks down latency per request.

  • DuckDB
  • Parquet
  • FastAPI
  • Locust
  • asyncio
